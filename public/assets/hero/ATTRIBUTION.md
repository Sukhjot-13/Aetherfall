# Hero assets

- `wayfarer.glb`: “Medieval guard captain, rigged + 10 animations”, CC0 1.0,
  https://vibeassets.io/a/9h7odik5fl (downloaded 2026-11-04).
- `locomotion.glb`: Walk and Run clips and rest skeleton extracted from “Rigged male
  camper in plaid flannel shirt (Meshy)”, CC0 1.0,
  https://vibeassets.io/a/zpepqtthci (downloaded 2026-11-04). Meshes and textures
  were removed; animations are retargeted to the hero at runtime.
- `actions.glb`: Jump and right-jab clips and rest skeleton extracted from “Rigged
  superhero in yellow and black suit (7 animations)”, CC0 1.0,
  https://vibeassets.io/a/y4hvexuc4t (downloaded 2026-11-04). Meshes, textures,
  unused clips, and translations were removed from the runtime asset.
